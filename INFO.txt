ABOUT
=================================================================================================
VROOM is a source-port of the DOOM engine focused on adding new features & polishing the original
code.VROOM is a fork of ozkl's doomgeneric port.
=================================================================================================

LICENSE
=================================================================================================
VROOM is protected by the GNU GPL v3.0. See LICENSE for more information about sharing.
=================================================================================================

PURPOSE
=================================================================================================
Originally, VROOM was just a fork that "fixed" things that I found unruly about the DOOM engine.
Nowadays, it has shifted to introducing completely new features not seen in the original game.
I am also aiming to fix bugs seen in the original DOOM.
=================================================================================================
