The VROOM source-port introduces custom sector types that expand
mapmaking capabilities beyond the original DOOM engine.

Below is a list of the sector types included in VROOM:

CASE 6 - INSTANT DEATH SECTOR
	Kills the player instantly upon contact.
	Doesn't work when God Mode is active.
	Great for simulating high fall damage.

CASE 15 - MUD SECTOR
	Slows the player down to a crawl.
	Monsters are not affected by this sector type.

CASE 18 - TRAMPOLINE (LOW)
	Sends the player upwards in the air.
	Doesn't affect monsters or things.
	Can be used for platforming puzzles/levels.

CASE 19 - TRAMPOLINE (HIGH)
	Does the same as CASE 18, with a higher bounce.

CASE 20 - HEALING SECTOR (10 HP)
	When standing inside this sector, the player is healed by 10 every 31 tics.
	This sector can only heal up to 100% health.

CASE 21 - HEALING SECTOR (20 HP)
	Same as case 20, but heals 20 hp instead of 10.

CASE 22 - ICE SECTOR
	Speeds the player up, while making the floor slippery.
	Good if used with a custom ice or snow texture.
	Just like mud, monsters aren't affected.
